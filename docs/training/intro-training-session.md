# CareCast Introductory Training Session
**Duration:** about 35 minutes
**Audience:** New family members
**Format:** Facilitator-led walkthrough (live demo recommended)
**Pairs with:** "Welcome to CareCast" video (2 min), a good thing to send before the session

---

## The Big Idea of This Session

Everything in CareCast fits into four steps. Say this early, and come back to it at each transition:

> **Set it up once → Let it run → Make it personal → Bring others in**

| Step | What it covers | Segment |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Set it up once** | Settings: profile, preferences, calendars, photos, routine, QR poster | 3 |
| **Let it run** | Autopilot builds and publishes every day | 4 |
| **Make it personal** | Page Editor, Personal Events, Reminders | 5–6 |
| **Bring others in** | Guest Greetings, Visitor Log | 7 |

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

- Explain what CareCast does and what their loved one sees on the Frame
- Sign in and move around the Family Portal
- Complete the one-time setup in Settings, including preferences and activity calendars
- Understand Autopilot, and know when a change needs to be published by hand
- Edit a day in the Page Editor and publish (or update) it
- Add Personal Events, including repeating ones, and create a Reminder
- Share a Guest Greetings link, approve a greeting, and find messages in the Visitor Log

## Segment 1: Welcome & What is CareCast? *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Keep this conversational. Connect emotionally before diving into features.

### Talking Points

**The Problem CareCast Solves**

> "When a loved one moves into senior living, staying connected can feel harder than it should. You can't always be there in person. Phone calls are short. And it's easy to feel like their day-to-day life is invisible to you, and yours is invisible to them."

**What CareCast Is**

CareCast has two parts you'll hear about all session:

1. **The Frame** is a display in your loved one's room. It has no buttons, no login and nothing for them to learn. It turns on and off on its own each day.
2. **The Family Portal** is the website where you (and other family members) manage what the Frame shows, from your phone or computer.

**What the Frame Shows**

Each day the Frame cycles through a set of pages:

| Page | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| **Info Page** | A note from family, a featured photo, and daily items like a joke or verse |
| **Day's Events** | Today's schedule: community activities plus personal events |
| **Upcoming Events** | A look ahead at what's coming soon (appointments, birthdays, holidays) |
| **Slideshow** | A rotating slideshow of family photos |

On top of those, **Reminders** and **Guest Greetings** appear when they're active.

**The Key Message**

> "You don't have to do anything every day. Once it's set up, CareCast builds and publishes your loved one's day for you. You step in only when you want to add something personal."

Then introduce the four steps: **set it up once, let it run, make it personal, bring others in.**

## Segment 2: Signing In & Finding Your Way Around *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Share your screen and walk through the portal live.

### Talking Points

**Signing In**
- Your first sign-in comes from the **invitation email**. Click the link and you're in, with no password to set up.
- After that, sign in at the CareCast login page with **Google**, or enter your email and click **Send sign-in link** to get a fresh link.
- You stay signed in on that device.

**The Navigation Bar (across the top)**

| Item | What It Does |
|------|-------------|
| **Frame Calendar** | Monthly view of every day. Start here to open any date. |
| **Page Editor** | Opens a day's pages for editing and publishing |
| **Personal Events** | Appointments, visits, and repeating activities for your loved one |
| **Reminders** | Gentle on-screen prompts at set times |
| **Guest Greetings** | Photo-and-note greetings from friends and extended family. A red badge means one is waiting for you. |
| **Visitor Log** | Messages left by visitors who scanned the QR code in the room |
| **Settings** | Resident Profile, Manage Calendar, Manage Photos and Icons, Visitor QR, Routine |
| **Help** | A built-in guide to every screen |

On a smaller screen, some of these items move into a **More** menu.

**The account menu** (top right) has **Send Feedback** and **Log Out**.

**Switching Residents**
- If you help with more than one person, click the **resident chip** in the header to switch.
- Everything you see is for the selected resident.

**Check-In: Any questions so far?**

## Segment 3: Set It Up Once: Settings *(7 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Frame this as "the 15 minutes that make everything else work." Most of it is done once and revisited only when something changes. Walk the Settings tabs in this order.

### 3a. Resident Profile *(Settings → Resident Profile)* *(3 min)*

**Display basics**
- **Timezone**
- **Active Hours:** when the screen is on (default 7:00 AM–9:30 PM)
- **Slide Duration:** how long each photo shows

After saving display changes, use **"Republish Today's Pages"** to apply them right away.

**Resident Preferences: the most important part of setup**

> "This is how CareCast knows which activities to pick for your loved one. The better the answers, the better the picks."

| Section | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Interests** | Love / Like / Neutral / Dislike / Not sure for music, faith, movies, games, crafts, outings, food socials and more |
| **Hard No's** | Categories to never pick, e.g. religious services, sports viewing, loud performances, group exercise, competitive games |
| **Other Considerations** | A free-text box for anything the questions don't cover, in your own words |

**Demonstrate Other Considerations** with an example like:
- *"Mom loves piano recitals but does not enjoy choirs."*
- *"Food events are fine, but Dad has diabetes, so avoid sweets."*

CareCast reads these notes when it picks events. Anything under **Hard No's**, or anything the notes say to avoid, is never placed on the Frame automatically.

Click **Save Preferences**.

### 3b. Activity Calendars *(Settings → Manage Calendar)* *(1.5 min)*

- Your community's activity calendars are loaded by CareCast. There's nothing to connect.
- **Activity Calendars at [facility]** lists every calendar at your loved one's community:
  - Their **home calendar** (their own unit) is always on.
  - **Tick any others** you'd like to see, for example **Campus Enrichment** or another unit's programs you might bring them to.
- Click **Save Changes**.

**What ticking a calendar does:**
- Its events appear in their own section of the Page Editor's event list (Segment 5), ready for you to add.
- On the Frame, those events show their source, e.g. *"Campus Enrichment · Chapel"*, so staff know the activity is off-unit.
- If an activity is on more than one calendar, it appears only once.
- Autopilot still uses **only the home calendar**. Events from added calendars go on the Frame only when you add them.

### 3c. Photos *(Settings → Manage Photos and Icons)* *(1 min)*
- Click **Upload Photos** and select images from your device.
- Upload a variety: family portraits, pets, trips, grandchildren. Landscape photos fill the screen best.
- Aim for 20 or more photos so the slideshow feels fresh.
- The **Icons** tab holds the pictures you can drag onto Day's Events.

### 3d. Routine *(Settings → Routine)* *(1 min)*
- Confirm it's set to **Autopilot** (the default). Segment 4 explains what it does.
- Set the **daily facility event target** (3–7) and the **auto-publish time**.

### 3e. Visitor QR *(Settings → Visitor QR)* *(0.5 min)*
- Click **Generate & Download PNG**, print it, and hang it somewhere visible in the room.
- It pays off in Segment 7.

**Check-In: Any questions about setup?**

## Segment 4: Let It Run: How Autopilot Builds Each Day *(4 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
This is where the anxiety drops. Most families never need to build a day from scratch.

### Talking Points

**Two Routine Modes** (*Settings → Routine*)

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Autopilot** (recommended, and the default) | CareCast prepares and publishes each day on a schedule. Your own edits for a day always take priority. |
| **Manual** | Nothing happens automatically. You build and publish every day yourself. **If you miss a day, the Frame has nothing new to show.** |

**What Autopilot Does Each Day**
- **Info Page:** starts from your saved default Info Page (Segment 5). It can rotate the featured photo and refresh widgets like the joke or Bible verse.
- **Day's Events:** adds your loved one's Personal Events, and picks community activities from their **home calendar** in one of three ways:
  - **Let AI pick events that match their interests** (recommended). CareCast picks the best matches first, then fills up to your daily target with other events. It never uses anything under **Hard No's** or anything your notes say to avoid.
  - Add all facility events. Use this with caution, because it can be a lot.
  - Add no facility events.
- **Upcoming Events:** pulls in birthdays, holidays, and upcoming appointments.
- **Slideshow:** keeps the same photos or picks a fresh random set daily.

**Other Routine Controls**
- **Auto-publish daily at:** the time each day goes out
- **Pause automation:** pause until a date, or indefinitely (for example, during a hospital stay)

**Tip:** If the AI's picks don't feel right, the fix is usually in **Settings → Resident Profile**: adjust interests, add a Hard No, or write a note in Other Considerations.

## Segment 5: Make It Personal: The Page Editor *(8 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
This is the heart of the session. Use a demo resident. Show the status bar changing color as you edit.

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

#### Step 1: Open a Day *(1 min)*
- Click **Frame Calendar** and click any date, or click **Page Editor** to jump straight to today.
- The Page Editor has four tabs: **Info Page**, **Day's Events**, **Upcoming Events**, and **Slideshow**.
- Each tab has a **"Show [Page] on today's frame"** toggle. Turn a page off and it won't be shown that day.
- Your work **saves automatically**. "Saving…" appears while it does.

#### Step 2: The Info Page *(3 min)*

A free-form canvas for your personal touch. Use the **ADD BLOCK** toolbar:

| Block | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Photo** | A featured photo. Drag one in from the **Photos** panel (top right). |
| **Text** | Your message. Double-click to edit, then pick a color and alignment. The text sizes itself automatically. |
| **Widget** | Content that fills itself in and refreshes daily: Joke, Bible Verse, Saying of the Day, Quote of the Day, or Word of the Day |

- Resize a block by dragging its bottom edge. Delete it with the red ×.
- If you see **"↕ Content clipped,"** make the block taller.

**Demonstrate:**
1. Add a **Text** block: *"Good morning, Mom! We love you and we'll call tonight."*
2. Add a **Widget** block → **Joke of the Day**. Show the ↻ refresh.
3. Check **"Use this as the default info page for future days."** Autopilot will now start every day from this layout. *This is the single most valuable thing to show.*

#### Step 3: Day's Events *(1.5 min)*
- Shows today's schedule. In Autopilot it's already filled in.
- The list on the left shows that day's personal and community events, grouped by calendar:
  - The **home calendar** group is open.
  - Each **added activity calendar** (Segment 3b) has its own group, closed to keep the list tidy. Click its name to open it.
- Click any event in the list to add it to the page.
- Click a title, time or description to edit it. Times like *"Morning"* or *"Anytime"* work too.
- Click the image area of an event, or drag an icon onto it, to change its picture.
- The **red ×** removes an event from *this day's page only*. It doesn't delete the event.

#### Step 4: Upcoming Events & Slideshow *(0.5 min)*
- **Upcoming Events:** a look-ahead list. Autopilot fills it in.
- **Slideshow:** photos come from *Settings → Manage Photos and Icons*. How long each photo shows is set in *Settings → Resident Profile* (Slide Duration).

#### Step 5: Read the Status Bar & Publish *(2 min)*

The line under the page tabs tells you where the day stands:

| Status bar | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Green** | Saved, and your loved one will see this day (now, or automatically on its date) |
| **Amber** | Saved, but **not on the Frame yet**. Click the button next to it. |

The button names the day it acts on, for example **"Publish Oct 13"**, **"Update Oct 6"**, or **"Update today's frame"**.

**Key Point to Emphasize:**
> "Autopilot publishes each day once. If you change a day that's already been published, including today, the bar turns amber, and you need to click **Update** to send your change. Until you do, the Frame keeps showing the earlier version. You can update as many times as you like."

**Check-In: Any questions about editing or publishing?**

## Segment 6: Make It Personal: Personal Events & Reminders *(4 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Both of these are "create once, CareCast handles the rest."

### Personal Events

Personal Events are your loved one's own schedule: appointments, family visits, haircuts, church, therapy.

**Adding an Event**
1. Click **Personal Events** in the navigation bar.
2. Add an event: **Title**, **Date**, **Start time**, optional **Notes**.
3. Set **Repeat** for regular activities (for example, every week on Tuesdays), with an optional end.

**How They Reach the Frame**
- In Autopilot, Personal Events are added to **Day's Events** automatically on their date, and upcoming ones show on **Upcoming Events**.
- You can also add them from the Day's Events list in the Page Editor.

**Changing or Removing**
- Double-click an event to edit it.
- Delete it on the Personal Events page. For a repeating event, you can cancel just one occurrence (say, skip one week) or the whole series.
- Remember: the red × on a Day's Events page only hides the event for that day.

### Reminders

Reminders put a gentle, full-screen prompt on the Frame during a time window you choose, for example *"It's time to take your morning medicine"* or *"Sarah is visiting today!"*

**Creating a Reminder**
1. Click **Reminders** in the navigation bar.
2. Write the **Message** and pick an **icon**.
3. Choose a **Display Style**. The **Live Preview** shows exactly how it will look.
4. Set the **Schedule**: a single date, or repeat on selected days each week. Then set **Show From** / **Show Until** times.
5. Save. Use **Preview** on any reminder to check it again later.

**Tip:** Keep messages short and warm. They're meant to be read at a glance.

## Segment 7: Bring Others In: Guest Greetings & the Visitor Log *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
These two features often delight families. They turn the wider circle of friends into participants.

### Guest Greetings

> "Friends, grandkids, old neighbors: anyone can send your loved one a photo and a short note, with no account needed. You stay in control of what appears."

**How It Works**
1. Go to **Guest Greetings** and **Copy** (or **Share**) your private link.
2. Send it to friends and family by text, email, or a group chat.
3. When someone sends a greeting, you get an **email**, and a red badge appears on **Guest Greetings**.
4. Review it under **Waiting for review**:
   - **Approve & Display**: choose to show it for 1, 2, or 3 days. It's added to the Frame's slideshow.
   - **Edit**: tidy up the message or name first.
   - **Decline**: it never appears.
5. Past greetings are kept under **Past greetings**.

**Link Control:** the link works until you change it. **Regenerate link** creates a new one (the old one stops working). Use it if the link has spread further than you'd like.

### The Visitor Log

> "Anyone who visits the room, such as a friend, a neighbor or a pastor, can scan the QR code poster and leave you a message. No app and no login."

- The poster comes from **Settings → Visitor QR** (Segment 3e).
- Open **Visitor Log** to read messages. A red badge shows new ones.
- Each message shows the visitor's name, their note, and when it was sent, newest first.

**Real-World Impact**
> "Imagine coming home from a long day and seeing: 'Your mom was so cheerful today. Pastor Jim stopped by and she sang along to every song.' These are the moments you'd otherwise miss."

## Segment 8: Wrap-Up: Your CareCast Rhythm *(3 min)*

### Closing Talking Points

Come back to the four steps one last time:

> "You've set it up once. Now here's all CareCast asks of you:"
> 1. **Nothing daily.** Autopilot keeps the Frame fresh every day.
> 2. **As life happens:** add Personal Events and Reminders.
> 3. **When you want to:** open a day, add a note or photo, and click **Update**.
> 4. **When greetings arrive:** approve them.
> 5. **Now and then:** revisit Preferences and Other Considerations if the activity picks need tuning.

**Getting Help**
- **Help** in the navigation bar explains every screen.
- **Send Feedback** in the account menu reaches the CareCast team directly.

**Open Q&A**

## Facilitator Checklist

Before the session:
- [ ] Confirm all participants have received their invitation email
- [ ] Have a demo resident ready (sandbox) with photos, a few Personal Events, a Reminder, and a Guest Greeting waiting for review
- [ ] Confirm the resident's facility calendars are loaded for this month (Admin → Facility Events coverage), including any extra activity calendars like Campus Enrichment
- [ ] Test screen sharing and audio
- [ ] Optionally send the "Welcome to CareCast" video ahead of time

During the session:
- [ ] Introduce the four steps in Segment 1 and return to them at each transition
- [ ] Check in for questions after Segments 2, 3, 5, and 7
- [ ] Allow participants to follow along in their own accounts if possible
- [ ] Encourage questions. First-time users often need reassurance.

After the session:
- [ ] Share the Quick-Reference Card
- [ ] Send a follow-up email with support contact info
- [ ] Schedule a 15-minute check-in 1 week later (a good time to review AI picks and adjust Preferences / Other Considerations together)
