# CareCast Introductory Training Session
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Audience:** New family members / caregivers
**Format:** Facilitator-led walkthrough (live demo recommended)

---

| Segment | Topic | Time |
|---------|-------|------|
| 1 | Welcome & What is CareCast? | 3 min |
| 2 | Your Dashboard & Navigation | 4 min |
| 3 | Creating Your First Daily Frame | 9 min |
| 4 | Calendar & Schedule Integration | 4 min |

| 6 | The Visitor Portal & QR Codes | 3 min |

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

- Explain what CareCast does and how it connects families with residents
- Navigate the CareCast dashboard confidently
- Create, preview, and publish a daily frame for a resident
- Understand how calendar events populate into frames
- Upload and manage resident photos
- Generate and share the visitor portal QR code
- Set up basic autopilot scheduling to reduce daily effort

## Segment 1 — Welcome & What is CareCast? *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Open with a brief welcome. Keep this segment conversational — connect emotionally before diving into features.

### Talking Points

**The Problem CareCast Solves**

> "When a loved one moves into a care facility, staying connected can feel harder than it should. You can't always be there in person. Phone calls are short. And it's easy to feel like your loved one's day-to-day life is invisible to you — and yours is invisible to them."

**What CareCast Is**

CareCast is a family communication platform with three connected parts:

1. **The Family App** (what you're using today) — where you create daily digital content for your resident
2. **The Frame** — a small LCD display in your resident's room that shows the content you create
3. **The Visitor Portal** — a webpage visitors can reach by scanning a QR code in the room, so they can leave messages for your family

**The Core Idea: Daily Frames**

> "Each day, you can publish a 'frame' — a curated set of three digital pages that display on your resident's screen. Think of it like a personalized daily newspaper just for them: who's thinking of them today, what's on their schedule, and a photo slideshow of their favorite memories."

**Key Concept: The Resident Is the Center**

Everything in CareCast revolves around the resident. You (the family member) are a manager of their experience.

### Facilitator Notes
Share your screen and walk through the app live. Point out each navigation area as you describe it.

### Talking Points

**Logging In**
- You received an invitation email with a magic link — clicking it logs you straight in, no password needed
- Once inside, you stay logged in on that device

**The Sidebar (Left Navigation)**

Walk through each item:

| Menu Item | What It Does |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Calendar** | Your main hub — see all days at a glance, create/edit frames |
| **Photos** | Upload and manage the resident's photo library |
| **Messages** | Read messages left by room visitors |
| **Routine** | Configure autopilot scheduling |

**Resident Selector**
- If you manage more than one resident, a dropdown at the top lets you switch between them
- Everything you see is specific to the selected resident

## Segment 3 — Creating Your First Daily Frame *(9 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
This is the heart of the session. Walk through the full create → preview → publish workflow. Use a real or demo resident.

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

#### Step 1: Open the Calendar *(1 min)*
- Click **Calendar** in the sidebar
- You'll see a monthly view with colored indicators on days that have frames
  - **Draft** (amber): frame created but not yet published
  - **Published** (green): frame sent to the display device
- Click on **today's date** (or any date) to open it

#### Step 2: Create a Frame *(1 min)*
- If no frame exists yet, click **"Create Frame"**
- The frame editor opens with three tabs at the top:
  - **Info Page**
  - **Schedule Page**
  - **Photos Page**

#### Step 3: Build the Info Page *(3 min)*

The Info Page is your personal message to the resident — what they see first.

**Widgets available on this page:**

| Widget | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Message** | A personal note from the family — "We're thinking of you today, Mom!" |
| **Notes** | General reminders or information |
| **Joke** | Auto-populated daily joke (can be refreshed) |
| **Bible Verse** | Daily devotional verse (optional) |
| **Photo** | A featured photo displayed prominently |

**Demonstrate:**
1. Click **"Add Widget"** → select **Message**
2. Type a short personal message: *"Good morning! We love you and are thinking of you today."*
3. Add a **Joke** widget — show how it auto-fills
4. Explain that widgets can be reordered by dragging

#### Step 4: Review the Schedule Page *(1 min)*
- Click the **Schedule** tab
- Any calendar events for this date are automatically listed here
- You can add or remove events manually
- This is what tells your resident what their day looks like

*(We'll cover calendar setup in the next segment)*

#### Step 5: Set Up the Photos Page *(1 min)*
- Click the **Photos** tab
- Select photos from the resident's library to include in today's slideshow
- Or choose "Use All Photos" to rotate through the full library
- Set how long each photo displays (default: 10 seconds)

#### Step 6: Preview the Frame *(1 min)*
- Click **"Preview Images"**
- CareCast renders each page as it will actually appear on the frame device
- Review all three pages — this is exactly what your resident will see
- Go back and adjust any content before publishing

#### Step 7: Publish *(1 min)*
- Click **"Publish"**
- CareCast sends all three pages to the resident's display
- The frame device picks up the update automatically — no one needs to be at the facility
- The calendar day turns **green** to confirm it's live

**Key Point to Emphasize:**
> "Publishing replaces whatever is currently on the screen — so if you need to make a correction, just edit and republish. There's no limit to how many times you can update."

**Check-In: Any questions about creating or publishing frames?**

## Segment 4 — Calendar & Schedule Integration *(4 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Explain how events get into the Schedule page automatically. This reduces daily effort significantly.

### Talking Points

**Two Types of Calendars**

1. **Personal Calendar** — the resident's own Google Calendar
   - Family members add appointments, visits, medical events
   - Example: *"Doctor's appointment at 2 PM"* or *"Family visit from Sarah"*

2. **Facility Calendar** — events provided by the care facility
   - Activities, group events, entertainment
   - Example: *"Music therapy at 3 PM"* or *"Bingo in the common room"*

**How to Connect a Calendar**
1. Go to **Settings → Calendars**
2. Click **"Connect Google Calendar"**
3. Sign in with the Google account that has the resident's calendar
4. Select which calendars to include

**How It Works in Frames**
- When you create a frame for a date, CareCast automatically pulls events from connected calendars for that day
- Events appear pre-populated on the Schedule page — you just review and adjust

**AI Event Matching (Assisted Mode)**
- CareCast can use AI to suggest which facility-wide events match the resident's personal interests and preferences
- This is configured under **Routine settings** (covered next)

## Segment 5 — Managing Photos *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
Photos are a core emotional driver of the product. Keep this segment warm and personal.

### Talking Points

**Why Photos Matter**
> "For many residents, looking at familiar faces and memories is one of the most comforting parts of their day. The photo slideshow runs continuously on the frame between page rotations."

**Uploading Photos**
1. Click **Photos** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Upload Photos"**
3. Select images from your device (supports JPG, PNG)
4. Photos are stored in the resident's library and available for all future frames

**Photo Tips**
- Upload a variety: family portraits, pets, vacations, grandchildren
- Landscape-oriented photos display best on the frame screen
- Aim for 15–30 photos to create a meaningful rotation

**Photo Rotation Options** (configured per frame or in Routine settings)
- **Daily rotation**: Show a fresh set each day
- **Random**: Randomly select from the library
- **Fixed**: Always show the same curated set

## Segment 6 — The Visitor Portal & QR Codes *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
This feature often surprises and delights family members. It turns passive visitors into active participants.

### Talking Points

**What Is the Visitor Portal?**
> "Anyone who visits your loved one's room — a friend, a neighbor, another family member, a pastor — can scan a QR code and leave a message for your family. No app download, no login required."

**How It Works**
1. A QR code is displayed in the resident's room (printed card or on the frame itself)
2. Visitor scans with their phone camera → a simple webpage opens
3. They enter their name and a message
4. You receive the message in the **Messages** section of the app

**Generating a QR Code**
1. Go to **Settings → Visitor Portal**
2. Click **"Generate QR Code"**
3. Download or print the QR code image
4. Place it somewhere visible in the room

**Reading Messages**
- Go to **Messages** in the sidebar
- New messages appear with the visitor's name, their message, and the timestamp
- Mark as read or archive when done

**Real-World Impact**
> "Imagine coming home from a long day and seeing: 'Your mom was so cheerful today — Pastor Jim stopped by and she sang along to every song.' These are the moments you'd miss otherwise."

## Segment 7 — Autopilot: Set It and Forget It *(3 min)*

### Facilitator Notes
For busy families, autopilot is a game-changer. Frame this as reducing guilt and cognitive load.

### Talking Points

**The Challenge Without Autopilot**
> "Life gets busy. Some families feel guilty when they miss a day of publishing a frame. Autopilot solves this."

**Three Routine Modes**

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Manual** | You create and publish every frame yourself |
| **Assisted** | System auto-drafts frames for you to review and approve |
| **Autopilot** | System creates AND publishes frames automatically every day |

**Setting Up Autopilot**
1. Click **Routine** in the sidebar
2. Choose your mode (start with **Assisted** if you're new)
3. Set your **Auto-Stage Time**: when the draft is created each morning (e.g., 7:00 AM)
4. Set your **Auto-Publish Time**: when it goes live (e.g., 8:30 AM)
5. Configure content rules:
   - **Info Page**: Keep stable? Refresh daily?
   - **Schedule Source**: Personal calendar only? Include facility events?
   - **Photos**: Rotate daily or keep fixed?
6. Click **Save**

**Recommendation for New Users**
> "Start with Assisted mode. Each morning you'll get a notification that a draft is ready. Spend 2 minutes reviewing it, add a personal message, and hit publish. As you get comfortable, switch to Autopilot for fully hands-free operation."
