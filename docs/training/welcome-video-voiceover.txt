When someone you love moves into senior living, it's easy to feel far from their day, and for them to feel far from yours. CareCast helps close that gap.

It starts with the Frame, a simple display in your loved one's room. There are no buttons and no logins, so there's nothing for them to learn. Every day it shows fresh pages: a note from family, today's schedule, and a slideshow of photos you've shared.

You manage it all from the Family Portal, on your phone or computer. Sign in with Google, or with a link we email you, so there's no password to remember.

Every day, CareCast puts together a fresh display. It pulls in your community's activities and highlights the ones that fit your loved one's interests. It also adds Personal Events you've entered, like a doctor's visit, weekly shower or family visit. Then it sends everything to the Frame automatically. Want to add a note or swap a photo? Open the day, make your change, and publish.

There are a few more ways to stay close. Reminders put gentle prompts on the screen at the right moment. Guest Greetings give friends and extended family a private link to send a photo and a short note. You approve it, and it appears on the Frame. And when visitors stop by, they can scan a code in the room to leave you a message in Messages.

You don't have to do anything every day, because CareCast keeps things fresh on its own. But whenever you add a message or a photo, your loved one sees it, right there in their room.

Welcome to CareCast. If you have questions, use Send Feedback in the menu. We're always listening.
